# Selected generation prompts

## Initial generation

Use case: stylized-concept

Create an authoring-only 3x3 Shield Drone sprite design sheet. Keep one compact upright small drone as the primary identity and one separate physical shield under 40 percent of the combined silhouette. Base the drone on Sector 01's clipped armor plates, 45-degree corners, and hexagonal or octagonal machinery housings. Show the same body with the shield at `E, SE, S, SW, W, NW, N, NE`, plus a neutral center cell. Use logical `32x32` low-resolution pixel art, a transparent background, hard pixel clusters, and no more than six opaque colors. Exclude energy rings, limbs, weapons, cables, dense panel noise, gradients, antialiasing, text, and watermarks.

## Selected refinement

Change only the drone body silhouette in all nine cells. Replace the tall rounded tablet body with one identical compact, slightly wider-than-tall clipped-octagonal industrial service housing. Use straight horizontal and vertical plate edges joined by unmistakable 45-degree cut corners. Preserve the 3x3 layout, shield shapes and positions, sensor, hover cue, palette, sparse pixel density, relative scale, and transparent background.
