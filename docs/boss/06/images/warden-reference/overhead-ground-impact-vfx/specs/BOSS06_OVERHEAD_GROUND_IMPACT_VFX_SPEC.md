# 1. Purpose

`overhead_ground_impact_vfx` is the impact effect for the Warden's
Overhead Slam attack.

The purpose is:

> Clearly communicate a heavy downward strike and danger zone.

This is different from Baton impact.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Gameplay Readability

Must communicate:

-   downward force
-   heavy impact

-   large attack commitment

Must be clearly different from:

## Baton Impact

-   small
-   direct hit
-   short electric spark

## Overhead Slam

-   large
-   ground-centered
-   heavy shock impact
