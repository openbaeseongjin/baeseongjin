# 1-7 ASSET REQUIREMENTS — REV8.0

## Actual game art identity

Compressed Pressure Bypass service chambers.

Actual art may include:
- pressure pipes
- valve assemblies
- gauge clusters
- shutters
- emergency vents
- bulkhead walls
- inspection ports
- narrow maintenance framing

## Gameplay-vs-preview separation

Actual art can use those background assets.

But `MAP-PREVIEW.html` only shows them if they affect gameplay.

SHOW:
- real Wind Zone
- real Wind Baffle
- real platforms
- real Grapple points
- Access Carrier / Guards
- Bypass interaction body

HIDE:
- background Pressure Valve Core
- decorative pipes
- decorative gauges
- cables
- scenery-only walls / braces

## Chamber contrast

Lower:
slightly wider / familiar.

Middle:
compressed, opposing pressure read.

Safe Shadow:
visually quiet / protected.

Upper:
longer rightward launch sightline.

Bypass:
clean safe control platform.

## Pressure Pulse language

Must match 1-6 Fan B:
- LULL
- WARNING
- ACTIVE
- DECAY

Do not create a new color or sound language.

The same WARNING sound should support two different decisions based on direction.

## Access Pocket

Compressed inspection pocket,
not a flat arena.

Carrier + Guards need readable local firing lines.

Do not visually reveal the whole enemy formation before commitment.
