# 1-8 ASSET REQUIREMENTS — REV8.0

## Actual game art identity

Sector 01 Containment Headhouse / final maintenance lock.

Actual art may contain:
- heavy containment doors
- lock actuators
- status rails
- maintenance control hardware
- pressure lines
- structural bulkheads
- deep gate machinery
- transition aperture toward Worker District

## Gameplay Preview separation

Actual art is NOT the Gameplay Map.

`MAP-PREVIEW.html` shows only:
- real collision
- Grapple
- enemy
- recovery
- real final Wind
- Override objective
- checkpoint
- route

Hide scenery-only:
- Gate mass
- pipes
- cables
- braces
- decorative panels
- Worker District background

## Security lane readability

Lower and Upper lanes should feel architecturally related,
but body direction must read differently.

Enemy activation:
current active threat must be visually dominant.

Do not make inactive future enemies visually louder than active threat.

## Mid Relief

Visually calmer:
- stable local lighting
- minimal telegraph clutter

Must support:
`LOCKDOWN · 87%`
and Player Bark.

## Final Vent

Use the established 1-6 / 1-7 Pulse language.

No new color grammar.

No blade-death language.

## Override

Panel is a clear maintenance device,
not a cinematic hero button.

Physical response:
- lock confirmation
- deep relay/contactors
- infrastructure disconnection sound

## Worker District reveal

Important:
do not reveal the entire Sector 02 map.

Gate opening should show only enough difference to communicate:
- this is no longer pure plant/service infrastructure
- an inhabited district exists beyond

System text and final audio handoff carry much of the reveal.

## Audio arc

Entry:
heavy locked Containment resonance.

Security:
late-pool cues, one dominant threat.

Mid:
combat drops out, closure mechanism/ticks.

Final Vent:
known Pulse.

Override:
local panel click → deep grid disconnect.

Gate:
heavy release.

Checkpoint:
maintenance ambience recedes,
inhabited district ambience begins.
