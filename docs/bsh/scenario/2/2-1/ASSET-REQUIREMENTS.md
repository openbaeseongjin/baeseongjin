# 2-1 ASSET REQUIREMENTS — REV8.1

## Identity

**LOW-RISE WORKER ROWHOUSES**

The Player diagonally cuts through several neighboring 2–3 storey residential modules.

Not one tall apartment block.

## Architectural sequence

1. Lower Alley
2. Small Court
3. Utility Landing
4. Laundry Landing
5. Upper Shared Gallery
6. Community Terrace

Each should feel like a fragment of ordinary residential circulation.

## Lived-in traces

Use:
- human-scale doors
- small windows
- laundry rail
- work jacket
- communal storage
- narrow exterior stair
- utility pipes
- chair
- plant
- meal tray
- community board

Place traces directly beside gameplay circulation.

## Architecture-to-gameplay causality

Grapple targets should visually resolve as:
- balcony underside
- stair frame
- residential support
- utility brace

No floating industrial Anchor look.

## Scale control

Do not show:
- giant upper skyline
- mega-atrium
- long empty vertical façade
- huge courtyard

Far background:
only modest housing depth.

Later Sector 02 Stages may expand scale.

## Laundry Offset

This is the only local direction correction.

Art should explain it:
- interrupted exterior stair
- façade obstruction
- laundry/utility landing

It must not look like an arbitrary platform zig-zag.

## Security

One old fixed Security unit.

Must not resemble a moving Patrol Drone.

## Notice

Community Notice is part of daily residential shared circulation.

Not command-center equipment.

## Lighting

Muted:
- old fluorescent
- small residual warm windows
- teal service light

Mostly dark/abandoned.

Not cozy active occupancy.

## Audio

- low residential utility hum
- pipes/drains
- loose railing
- laundry movement
- one fixed Security cue
- small Notice alert

Do not use full Patrol Drone movement grammar before 2-2.
