# 2-4 ASSET REQUIREMENTS — REV8.0

## Identity
**INTERLOCKED LOW-RISE WORKER TENEMENT COURTS**

Scale comes from many overlapping 2–4 storey housing fragments,
not a high-rise tower.

Use:
- narrow balconies
- exterior galleries/stairs
- courtyard bridges
- utility frames
- many small doors
- laundry
- utility shelves
- canteen crates
- small plants
- waiting benches
- repair patches
- resident-specific small markers

Most windows dark; sparse residual warm/old fluorescent light.

## Gameplay readability
Final art must not literally color-code Safe/Flow/Pressure.
Player infers style from landing width, directness, LOS, angle and Recovery.

Grapple points should read as:
- balcony underside
- gallery frame
- stair support
- utility bridge joint
- residential structural brace

## Story restraint
Optional neutral child drawing is allowed only as ordinary lived-in trace.
No HELP / SAVE US / blood / death shorthand.

Do not show 2-5 evacuation status text early.

Priority:
Gameplay readability > enemy readability > Story > decoration.
