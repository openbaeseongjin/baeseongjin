# 2-5 ASSET REQUIREMENTS — REV8.0

## Core visual identity

**PUBLIC EVACUATION INFRASTRUCTURE THAT NARROWS INTO A LOCKED TRANSIT TERMINUS,
THEN BREAKS INTO NON-CIVILIAN MAINTENANCE ACCESS.**

The social transition must be legible without text.

## Public Assembly Concourse

Wide, orderly, civic.

Possible traces:
- queue rails
- temporary chairs
- water containers
- bags
- folded blankets
- worker ID tags
- assembly floor markings

Do not make it look like panic.

## Broken Queue Spans

Gameplay-relevant breaks should feel like damaged evacuation circulation:
- broken walkway joints
- missing bridge plate
- fractured queue terrace
- exposed structural Grapple members

Avoid decorative platform-looking background geometry.

## Transit Neck

Visually narrow.

This is where crowd-scale public circulation becomes compromised.

Assembly Guard silhouette and next Grapple target must remain readable.

## Gate Forecourt

The visual center of Story.

Need:
- sealed Upper Transit Gate
- evacuation status display
- orderly waiting traces
- broad safe floor

Do not show:
- bodies
- blood
- HELP
- SAVE US
- farewell notes

The unsettling fact is procedural:
people assembled here and the process stalled.

## Upper Transit Gate

Must feel:
- public
- large enough for mass circulation
- physically sealed
- inactive / denied

Do not add an obvious Player override control.

Do not visually imply it will open in this Stage.

## Service Hatch / Maintenance route

Strong visual contrast from the public Gate:
- narrow aperture
- raw steel/service framing
- cable tray
- no public guardrail
- no queue width
- no residential/civilian circulation cues

It should be obvious why a Grapple-equipped maintenance worker can continue
while a crowd could not.

## Commit Drop targets

Resolve as:
- internal service-frame cross member
- maintenance brace
- cable-tray hanger
- shaft joint

Make them high contrast enough to read during fall.

## Recovery dividers

Must look like real shaft partitions / service walls.

Their purpose is gameplay:
a missed catch cannot simply walk forward.

## Access B Alcove

Small side service bay.

Carrier marker remains Runtime UI:
edge arrow → diamond.

Do not add a text label.

No escort staging.

## Lighting

Public Concourse:
old public fluorescent / emergency transit lighting.

Forecourt:
slightly clearer status/Gate readability, not cinematic spotlight.

Service Drop:
cooler, harsher maintenance light; sparse utility indicators.

## Audio

Public:
PA hum + Transit machinery + known Security cues.

Safe Story:
Security drops out; only Gate/status ambience.

Drop:
metal frame / Rope / falling air / service hum.

Carrier:
current combat + Access marker cues.

Exit:
quiet residential ambience toward 2-6.

Priority:
Gameplay readability > Security readability > Story > decoration.
