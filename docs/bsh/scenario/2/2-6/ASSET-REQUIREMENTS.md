# 2-6 ASSET REQUIREMENTS — REV8.0

## Identity

**QUIET LOW-RISE RESIDENTIAL COURTYARD RIM**

This Stage is the visual breath after 2-5.

The scale reveal comes from:
- many low-rise housing fragments
- near / mid / far depth layers
- deep central courtyard void
- repeated human-scale doors and balconies

not from a giant high-rise atrium.

## Entry Lift

Keep visually narrow and service-like,
as a continuation from the low 2-5 maintenance exit.

As Player climbs:
residential materials gradually replace transit/service materials.

## Reveal Overlook

This is the most important visual beat.

Need:
- safe broad foreground Gallery
- nearby housing façade
- opposite housing façade
- farther Block layer
- central downward courtyard depth
- readable continuation to the right

The Player should think:
**there are far more homes here than the previous rooms implied.**

## Lived-in traces

Allowed:
- many small doors
- narrow balconies
- laundry
- plants
- chairs
- shared shelves
- repair patches
- small utility boxes
- sparse weak warm lights
- old fluorescent service lights

Most rooms/windows remain dark.

No residents visible.

## Silence

Do not communicate silence through horror clichés.

Avoid:
- bodies
- blood
- destroyed toys as tragedy shorthand
- HELP / SAVE US
- farewell notes

Use normal objects in an abnormally empty place.

## Courtyard Void

Background scale prop only.

Possible:
- stacked opposite façades
- deep service shafts
- vertical laundry lines
- distant gallery lights

Do not accidentally draw:
- false playable platforms
- false Grapple nodes
- Sector 03 architecture
- giant open sky that breaks Worker District enclosure

## Gameplay foreground

Only actual gameplay objects should read as traversal:
- Lift Landing
- Reveal Overlook
- Quiet Rim
- Gap A landing
- Rim Transfer
- Gap B landing
- Exit

Grapple targets:
- balcony underside
- gallery support
- residential structural joint

## Guard readability

Guard A and B are late.

Environment detail must not hide:
- their silhouette
- current attack cues
- landing edges
- G3/G4

They should feel like residual Security embedded in housing,
not a new combat arena.

## Lighting

Reveal:
mostly dark residential mass,
sparse warm windows,
old cool fluorescent circulation light.

Enemy bands:
small return of Security light/cues.

Exit:
public Shelter-access language may begin to appear architecturally,
but no 2-7 text/status.

## Audio

Entry:
service hum fading.

Reveal:
wind / ventilation / light material movement / distant building mechanical sound.

No dramatic music sting.

Guard A/B:
short local Security cues.

Exit:
distant Shelter machinery begins,
without 2-7 status announcement.

Priority:
Gameplay readability > enemy readability > Story atmosphere > decoration.
