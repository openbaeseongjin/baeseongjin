# 3-1 ASSET REQUIREMENTS — REV8.0

## Identity

**LARGE POWERED LOWER MARKET + SUSPENDED CENTRAL MARKET ISLAND**

The visual purpose is not luxury.

It is:
**ordinary daily commercial life at a much larger scale, still partially powered, but empty.**

## Left Market

Need:
- everyday shopfronts
- pharmacy / laundry / repair / meal / work-supply cues
- some powered signs/displays
- some damaged/dark bays
- broad public terrace

## Central Suspended Market Island

Must read as actual architecture, not a floating game platform.

Need:
- hanging concourse structural frame
- former public bridge/escalator connection evidence
- broad 512px-class playable deck
- Welcome Kiosk
- multiple shop/service faces
- visible support members that explain Grapple joints

This is the Stage's visual landmark.

## Right Market

Need:
- ordinary commercial terrace
- small security/service point for the one pooled Guard
- clear projectile/telegraph contrast if Artillery appears

## Damage restraint

Allowed:
- cracked panel
- fallen sign
- dark storefront
- abandoned bag
- stalled door
- partial emergency lighting

Do not make:
- perfect untouched mall
- luxury fantasy
- corpse/blood scene
- VIP-coded space
- Group A/B class-coded environment

## Scanner foreshadow

Near Exit only:
small inactive service-mount / scanner housing silhouette may appear.

No:
- beam
- state text
- alarm
- active color cycle
- attach denial

## Lighting

Brighter than Worker District, but mixed.

Use:
- warm white
- muted gold
- muted magenta
- desaturated teal
- cool concrete/navy structure

Gameplay:
Rope Cyan remains highest-priority cyan.

Commercial signage must not visually compete with Grapple targets.

## Gameplay Map rule

Do not add decorative objects to MAP-PREVIEW unless they:
- collide
- grapple
- block LOS
- recover
- present Story
- spawn/host enemy
- gate progress
