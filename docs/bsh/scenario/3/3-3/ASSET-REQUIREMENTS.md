# 3-3 ASSET REQUIREMENTS — REV8.0

## Architectural identity
Central Retail Atrium with **alternating broken escalator banks**.

The art must explain every direction reversal:
- first escalator rises right
- next bank returns left
- broken public continuation exposes a lower central service gantry
- upper surviving bridge returns left
- final connector exits right

## Gameplay landmarks
- Lower Retail landing
- East Mid landing
- West Observation Balcony
- Central Hanging Service/Wayfinding Gantry
- West Upper Bridge
- Exit Mezzanine

## Scanner
Only C1/C2 on central descending gantry.
Do not visually place active Scanner systems elsewhere.

## Patrol
Patrol volume is central Atrium security band.
Readable against background.

## Gameplay Map rule
Decorative escalator skin, shop signs, ads, railings, plants, furniture and trusses remain out unless gameplay-functional.
