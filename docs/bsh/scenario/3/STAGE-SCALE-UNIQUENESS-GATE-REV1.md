## Similarity gate

Each Stage must differ from earlier stages in at least three meaningful dimensions:

- silhouette
- movement axis or direction rhythm
- Rope rhythm
- failure direction
- Scanner relationship
- enemy pressure
- route decision
- access approach
- augment expression

Verdict: 0–1 meaningful overlap = PASS; 2 = REVIEW; 3 or more = REDESIGN.
