## 1. One-line definition

A large elevated refuge terrace wraps around a protected central ascent core.
The player keeps gaining height through an outer terrace ascent under persistent pursuit,
breaks pursuit at a sealed Refuge Airlock, then chooses between:

- a longer safe outer bypass, or
- a shorter Inner Security Spur containing Override C under one late static security threat.

Both paths merge into a threat-free upper Refuge Control Deck.
The stage confirms only that the upper refuge route and protected ascent remain actively secured.
