
The two vertical Bus Risers are linked by authenticated cross-connect galleries.

```text

↑

RIGHT BUS

LEFT BUS
↑
CENTRAL CONVERGENCE
```

At the top, both buses terminate at the Authority Record Deck.

Architecture and story match:
