## 1. Purpose

Sector 06 begins by removing the spatial support the player has relied on for five sectors.

No new mechanic.
No new threat.
No new input.

`ROOM / SHAFT / ATRIUM` is over.

`ROOFTOP ISLAND → MAST BRACKET → ANTENNA SERVICE PLATFORM → CROWN DECK`
